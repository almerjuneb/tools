Create one polished logo presentation sheet containing five original, coordinated brand marks: AJB Personal Toolkit, TableCraft, File Converter, Image Studio, and QR Studio.

COMPOSITION
Use a clean, landscape 4:3 canvas with a transparent background if supported; otherwise use a plain warm-white background. Place the AJB Personal Toolkit identity centered across the top. Arrange the four tool logos below in a balanced two-column by two-row grid. Keep generous, even gutters so each logo can later be cropped separately. Each tile should contain one distinctive symbol and its exact wordmark. Keep all marks fully inside the canvas with no overlaps or cut-off edges.

BRAND DIRECTION
Make all five marks feel like one thoughtful personal software suite: precise geometric construction, confident simple silhouettes, crisp vector-like edges, restrained detail, and excellent small-size legibility. Use a shared palette of deep charcoal, fresh lime, and clear teal, with white or warm-white as needed for contrast. Give each tool its own recognizable accent treatment while keeping the set visibly related. Use clean contemporary lettering and reproduce the names below exactly. Do not add taglines, extra labels, decorative captions, gradients, mockups, device frames, or background scenery. Do not imitate existing company logos or copyrighted marks.

THE FIVE IDENTITIES
1. AJB Personal Toolkit: an original, memorable AJB monogram paired with the exact words "AJB PERSONAL TOOLKIT". This is the personal umbrella identity and should feel confident, practical, and welcoming.
2. TableCraft: a compact table or spreadsheet grid with one subtle crafted detail, paired with the exact word "TableCraft". It represents clean, organized reporting.
3. File Converter: a document-page symbol transforming into another format, paired with the exact words "FILE CONVERTER". Keep the transformation clear without using familiar office-software logos.
4. Image Studio: a simple image-frame or aperture-inspired mark with a restrained editing/detail cue, paired with the exact words "IMAGE STUDIO". Avoid generic AI sparkles as the main symbol.
5. QR Studio: a distinctive arrangement of modular square marks suggesting QR design, paired with the exact words "QR STUDIO". Keep it graphic and logo-like rather than presenting a real scannable code.

Prioritize exact spelling, strong contrast, consistent visual weight, and clean separation between the five marks. The final result must be one cohesive image, not five separate files.
